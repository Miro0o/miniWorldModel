## Conferences

> [!tip] Reading these PL conference summaries
> Counts below refer to the main research-paper/PACMPL track, not workshops, SRC abstracts, journal-first talks, or invited papers. For 2023–2026, **PLDI, POPL, OOPSLA, and ICFP publish in PACMPL**; PLDI joined PACMPL in 2023. OOPSLA has two annual issues/rounds, so comparisons must retain the original submission and revision denominator. See the [SIGPLAN PACMPL overview](https://www.sigplan.org/PACMPL/). These venues normally give several **ACM SIGPLAN Distinguished Paper Awards**, not one best paper. Start with the award-badged papers, then use the program sessions to follow one theme—compilers, verification, semantics/types, synthesis, runtimes, or language design—across years.
>
> **Format at a glance:** accepted research papers receive oral presentations; PLDI/POPL week and SPLASH also contain workshops, tutorials, keynotes, artifact evaluation, and an ACM Student Research Competition whose first stage is a poster session. OOPSLA papers are published in the year's PACMPL issue and presented at SPLASH, whose companion program also includes posters, demos, a doctoral symposium, and Onward!.

> **Award-list scope (checked 2026-09-26):** main-track Distinguished Paper Awards, with full titles and paper/program links. Artifact awards, SRC awards, and retrospective Most Influential Paper awards are separate categories. A list marked incomplete below is not a claim that other winners do not exist.

## Reading the awards: writing and research themes

下列是基于题名和论文类型的**阅读分组**，不是整个领域的主题占比统计。先读论文，再判断其贡献是否适合自己的问题。

| 阅读线索 | 可串读的获奖论文 | 写作时重点拆解 |
|---|---|---|
| 所有权、并发与验证 | *Tree Borrows* (PLDI 2025); *Data Race Freedom à la Mode* (POPL 2025); *VerusBelt* (PLDI 2026) | 从具体不安全行为到语义模型，再到定理、实现与适用边界，怎样形成完整证据链。 |
| 概率程序与推断 | *ADEV* (POPL 2023); *Error Credits* (ICFP 2024); *Guaranteed Bounds on Posterior Distributions…* (POPL 2025) | 精确定义概率保证、近似误差和假设；区分数学结论与实验现象。 |
| 编译与搜索 | *Mosaic* / *CryptOpt* (PLDI 2023); *Minotaur* (OOPSLA 2024); *Bonsai* (PLDI 2026) | 为什么现有表示或优化器不够；新表示带来的能力如何通过消融和工作负载验证。 |
| 人与工具 | *Grounded Copilot* (OOPSLA 2023); *Profiling Programming Language Learning* (OOPSLA 2024) | 研究问题、参与者与任务选择、编码方法、外部有效性如何交代。 |
| 生成与测试 | *Reflecting on Random Generation* (ICFP 2023); *Programmable Property-Based Testing* (ICFP 2026); *Enumerating Ill-Typed Programs…* (PLDI 2026) | 怎样定义测试覆盖与发现能力，避免只用样例数量证明方法有效。 |

每读一篇，记录六项：**问题缺口、关键例子、核心洞察、技术难点、证据、局限**。跨年比较同一问题的假设和评价标准，比仅比较热门关键词更能帮助选题。
