## Conferences

> [!tip] Reading these software-engineering conference summaries
> Counts are for the **main research track** only; industry, NIER/new-ideas, journal-first, demos/tools, datasets, doctoral symposium, and workshop papers have separate review pools. Since 2024–2026 several venues use dual cycles or major revision, so “submitted” means papers entering those research cycles. The usual paper honor is a set of **ACM SIGSOFT Distinguished Paper Awards**, not a single best paper; the explicitly enumerated lists below cover this research-paper award, excluding artifact-only awards, other tracks, and retrospective/impact awards. Sources are official programs and award lists, cross-checked on **2026-09-26**. A missing list is marked explicitly; it does not mean that no award exists.
>
> **Format at a glance:** main research papers are presented orally in parallel sessions. The broader conference programs add keynotes, journal-first talks, industry tracks, posters and ACM Student Research Competition, tool/demo and dataset tracks, artifact evaluation/badges, doctoral symposia, tutorials, and workshops. ISSTA focuses most tightly on testing and program analysis; ASE on automation; FSE on foundations and methods; ICSE spans the full field.

### Reading guide: 写作与研究方向

以下是基于上方名单的**阅读建议和主题观察**，不是对全部收录论文做过统计后的 trend 结论。论文数量受会议规模、奖项比例和选奖标准影响；判断方向增长还应检查各年完整 proceedings，并以全部研究论文为分母。

| 阅读目标 | 可从哪些获奖论文开始 | 阅读时记录什么 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 方法论文如何交代关键想法 | FSE 2023 *Baldur*；ICSE 2025 *Rango* | 旧方法在哪一步失败？核心机制如何对应这个失败？关键模块如何通过消融验证？ |
| 如何写有说服力的评测或反例 | ISSTA 2025 *Top Score on the Wrong Exam*；ISSTA 2026 *Re-evaluating Detection of Equivalent Mutants Using LLMs* | benchmark 的假设是什么？划分、泄漏、指标和真实任务之间是否存在差距？ |
| 实证研究如何形成解释 | ICSE 2024 *Breaking the Flow*；FSE 2026 *A Grounded Theory of Debugging in Professional Software Engineering Practice* | RQ、抽样、数据收集、分析方法、结论和有效性威胁如何对应？ |
| LLM 工具如何走向软件工程 agent | FSE 2025 *Demystifying LLM-based Software Engineering Agents*；ASE 2025 *Understanding Software Engineering Agents*；ICSE 2026 *SEAlign* | 如何定义任务边界、工具交互、轨迹和失败原因？评测是模型级还是项目级？ |
| 软件安全与传统分析如何持续演进 | ASE 2023 *Merge-Replay*；FSE 2024 *ProveNFix*；ISSTA 2025 *BinDSA* | 精度、性能、健全性假设和真实漏洞发现如何取舍？不要只看最终提升百分比。 |
| 如何评估研究的持续价值 | TOSEM 2025、2026 TOP Award 两篇论文 | 比较原论文提出的问题、五年后的适用范围与官方获奖访谈，区分短期热度与持续影响。 |

建议给每篇精读论文写一页笔记：**问题与场景 → 最接近的已有工作 → 关键缺口 → 核心想法 → 支撑每个主张的实验 → 局限与可复用的写作组织方式**。先看图 1、Introduction 和实验问题，再细读方法与威胁分析；学习论证结构时，也检查它是否适合自己的研究问题。
