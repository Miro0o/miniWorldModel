## Intro
> 🔗 https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rocq

The **Rocq Prover** (formerly named **Coq**) is an [interactive theorem prover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proof_assistant "Proof assistant") first released in 1989. It allows the expression of mathematical assertions, mechanical checking of proofs of these assertions, assists in finding [formal proofs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_proof "Formal proof") using proof automation routines and extraction of a certified program from the [constructive proof](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constructive_proof "Constructive proof") of its [formal specification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_specification "Formal specification").

Rocq works within the theory of the calculus of inductive constructions, a derivative of the [calculus of constructions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calculus_of_constructions "Calculus of constructions"). Rocq is not an [automated theorem prover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automated_theorem_prover "Automated theorem prover") but includes automatic theorem proving tactics ([procedures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subroutine "Subroutine")) and various [decision](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decidability_\(logic\) "Decidability (logic)") procedures.

The [Association for Computing Machinery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_for_Computing_Machinery "Association for Computing Machinery") awarded [Thierry Coquand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thierry_Coquand "Thierry Coquand"), [Gérard Huet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G%C3%A9rard_Huet "Gérard Huet"), [Christine Paulin-Mohring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christine_Paulin-Mohring "Christine Paulin-Mohring"), Bruno Barras, Jean-Christophe Filliâtre, Hugo Herbelin, Chetan Murthy, Yves Bertot, and Pierre Castéran with the 2013 [ACM Software System Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACM_Software_System_Award "ACM Software System Award") for Rocq (when it was named Coq).
